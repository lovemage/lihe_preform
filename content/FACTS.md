## Products (products-data.json)

- PET preform molds: 2 to 160 cavities; over 12,000 cavities produced per year; neck finishes PCO 1881, PCO 1810, 1716, 3025, 2925, 2622, 2522, 38mm, 45mm, 68mm, 80mm, 90mm wide-mouth, ROPP and custom; machines HUSKY, NETSTAL, SACMI, SIPA, Milacron and others.
- Tooling conversion: replacement parts made from customers' existing Husky/SACMI/Otto parts; cooling and blowing-condition optimization to shorten cycles; neck finish conversions and lightweighting.
- Hot runners: valve gate, up to 144 cavities (preform) and 96 cavities (closure); compatible with HUSKY, NETSTAL, KM, SIPA; replacement nozzle tips, valve pins, heaters, seals and refurbishment of existing hot runners.
- Closure injection molds: 12 to 72 cavities; one-piece and two-piece caps, tamper-evident bands, EU tethered caps; 26mm, 28mm, 2925, 3025, 38mm.
- Compression molds: for SACMI CCM lines; lower processing temperature → shorter cooling, shorter cycles, lower energy than injection; replacement tooling from existing parts.
- Blow molds: two-step SIDEL, KRONES, SIG/KHS, TECH-LONG, NEWAMSTAR; one-step SIPA, ASB, AOKI; cold fill, hot fill (about 85–95°C; standard PET deforms above about 60°C), aseptic; KHS InnoPET Blomax and Krones Contiform cold fill sets; split-type quick-change molds; water bottles 330ml, 500ml, 550ml, 1.5L.
- Accessories: blowing pins, stretch rods, clamping bases, mold shells, conversion parts (star wheels, guides, neck grippers).
- Mold bases: machined on Mazak, Makino, Haas; ground on Okamoto; verified on Zeiss CMM; for preform molds up to 160 cavities.
- Machining: Mazak, Makino, Okamoto (Japan); Haas, Hardinge (USA); Beijing Jingdiao; Sodick EDM.
- QC: Zeiss CMM, 3D profile scanner, optical projector, bottle sample inspection.
